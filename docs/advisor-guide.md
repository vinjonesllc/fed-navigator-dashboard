# Fed Navigator — Advisor Guide
### Turning your workshop report into booked appointments

Your Fed Navigator dashboard isn't a stats page to admire — it's a **warm-lead list**. Every person in it raised their hand to attend a federal retirement workshop, and the report tells you *who is closest to retiring, who is most engaged, and exactly what each person asked about.* That's everything you need to reach out with a reason.

This guide walks you through what you're looking at and, more importantly, **what to do after you have the report** to book more appointments.

---

## The 60-second read (do this first, every time)

When a new workshop report is ready, look at three things in this order:

1. **"Retiring soon" panel** — the people retiring within the next 6–12 months. *These are your appointments this week.*
2. **"Requested cliff notes" panel** — people who asked for the summary/materials. They literally asked you for something. *Reply today.*
3. **The Q&A and chat** — what people actually asked. This is your opening line for every call and email.

Everything else (attendance %, retention curve, ratings) tells you how the *workshop* performed. The three items above tell you who to *call.*

## Your dashboard, panel by panel

### Advisor overview (your home page)

| What you see | What it means | How to use it |
|---|---|---|
| **Next workshop** card(s) | Your upcoming session(s), with a live **registrant count** and a **Download registrations** button | Know your pipeline. Download the registrant list to prep outreach *before* the event. |
| **Workshops / Total attendees / Average attendance** | All-time totals across your sessions | Track your trend. Rising attendance % means your promotion + reminders are working. |
| **Average rating** | Average of each workshop's star rating | Your credibility signal. A 4.5★+ average is a great line in your follow-up: *"attendees rated this 4.7 out of 5."* |
| **Workshops table** | Every session — click any row to open its full report | Your archive. Old workshops are still full of people who haven't retired *yet.* |

### The workshop report (click any workshop)

| Section | What it tells you | The appointment angle |
|---|---|---|
| **Attendance funnel** | Registered → attended → stayed | Context for your numbers; not a call list by itself. |
| **Retention curve** | How many stayed minute-by-minute | People who stayed to the **end** are your hottest prospects — they sat through 2.5+ hours voluntarily. |
| **Live attendees** | Everyone who attended — **click a name** for their full profile | Your master list. The detail pop-up is where the gold is (see below). |
| **Q&A** | Every question asked, with who asked it | Personalize outreach: *"You asked about survivor benefits — let's walk through your numbers."* |
| **Retiring soon** | Attendees signaling retirement within 6–12 months | **Tier-1 leads. Call first.** |
| **Requested cliff notes** | People who asked for the summary/materials | **High intent. They asked you for something — respond.** |
| **What attendees said** | Their evaluation comments + ratings | Real quotes you can reference, and warm fuzzies that justify a call. |

### The attendee detail pop-up (click any name)

This is the single most useful screen for personalizing outreach. For one person it shows:

- **Time in session** — how long they stayed (longer = warmer)
- **Chats** — every message they typed, in their words
- **Questions** — what they asked
- **Evaluation** — their rating and comment, if they left one

> **Why it matters:** You never have to make a cold "just following up" call again. You can open with *their* concern, in *their* words.

## Who to contact first — the priority tiers

Don't work the list top-to-bottom. Work it by **heat:**

**🔥 Tier 1 — Call within 48 hours**
- **Retiring within 6 months** (the "Retiring soon" panel, labeled by date/month)
- Anyone who asked a **personal, specific question** ("my last day is in August — what about my FEHB?")

**🟠 Tier 2 — Call this week**
- **Retiring within 6–12 months** (including the "Possibly…" ones — tentative still means thinking about it)
- **Cliff-notes requesters** — fulfill the request, then offer a conversation

**🟡 Tier 3 — Email this week, call next**
- **Stayed to the end** of the workshop but gave no timeline
- **Highly engaged** — multiple chats or questions (they're processing, not just watching)

**🔵 Tier 4 — Nurture**
- Everyone else who attended. They're not cold — they showed up. Add them to your newsletter/drip.

> Use the attendee pop-up to confirm heat before you dial: someone who stayed 10 minutes and said nothing is a different call than someone who stayed the whole time and asked three questions.

## The post-report playbook

### Step 1 — Same day: fulfill every explicit request
Anyone in **"Requested cliff notes"** asked you for something. Send it the same day, personally (not an autoresponder). That email *is* your opener:

> *"Hi [Name] — here are the cliff notes from yesterday's session as you requested. While I have you: a lot of folks had follow-up questions about their own numbers. If it'd help, I keep a few slots open for workshop attendees — happy to grab one for you. [scheduling link]"*

### Step 2 — Within 48 hours: call your Tier-1 list
Open with **their** question (pull it from the Q&A or their attendee pop-up):

> *"Hi [Name], this is [Advisor] — I presented Thursday's federal retirement workshop. You asked about [survivor benefits / the FERS supplement / your TSP withdrawal options]. That's exactly the kind of thing that's easier to walk through with your actual numbers in front of us. Do you have a few minutes this week?"*

If no answer, leave a voicemail **and** follow with a text/email referencing the same question. (Texting only those who **opted in** — the registrant export flags this.)

### Step 3 — This week: email Tier 2 & 3
Reference the workshop, the rating, and a relevant resource. Keep it short and make the ask explicit:

> *"Thanks for joining [date]'s workshop — attendees rated it [4.7]★. You mentioned you're looking at retiring [next spring]. The window before you go is when the planning matters most. I'd like to put together a quick, no-obligation snapshot for you — when's good for a quick call?"*

### Step 4 — Ongoing: work the back catalog
Open older workshops in the **Workshops table.** Someone who was "12 months out" six months ago is now **6 months out.** Re-run that list quarterly — your past reports are a renewable pipeline.

## Using the exports

Three download buttons turn the dashboard into something you can work in your CRM or phone:

- **Export All** (on a workshop report) — every attendee with all their details (contact, agency, age, opt-ins, every question they asked, attended Y/N). Drop it into your CRM and tag the Tier-1/2 people.
- **Download registrations** (on the Next workshop card) — the sign-up list for an *upcoming* session. Use it to pre-call or pre-email registrants and lift attendance.
- **Download evaluations** — the feedback/ratings. Mine the comments for testimonials and for the topics people want more of.

> Tip: filter your CRM by **agency** (it's in the export). A batch of VA or USPS attendees lets you tailor one message about their specific benefit quirks and send it to a dozen people at once.

## A simple weekly rhythm

| When | Do |
|---|---|
| **Day of report** | Send cliff notes / fulfill requests. Skim Q&A for your talking points. |
| **Within 48 hrs** | Call every Tier-1 (retiring ≤6 months + specific question). VM + text/email each. |
| **By end of week** | Email Tier 2 & 3. Add everyone to your nurture list. |
| **Following week** | Second touch on no-answers (new angle, new question of theirs). |
| **Quarterly** | Re-open past workshops; re-work anyone whose timeline has now arrived. |

Most appointments come from the **second and third touch**, not the first — the dashboard gives you a fresh, *honest* reason to reach out each time (a different question they asked, a resource, their stated timeline).

## Reading the health metrics (and improving them)

- **Average attendance %** — registered vs. who showed. Low? Tighten your reminder emails/texts and confirm the calendar invite. The **Download registrations** list is your reminder audience.
- **Retention curve** — a steep early drop means the opening didn't hook; a long tail means strong content. The people on the *tail* are your warmest leads.
- **Average rating** — your social proof. Quote it in outreach. Watch the comment themes for what to cover next time.

---

## A few do's and don'ts

- **Do** lead with their words. The whole point of the report is that you never have to guess what they care about.
- **Do** move fast on the retiring-soon and cliff-notes lists — intent fades within days.
- **Don't** blast the same generic email to everyone; segment by tier and agency.
- **Don't** text anyone who didn't opt in (the export flags opt-in status).
- **Do** follow your firm's compliance rules on outreach, recordkeeping, and what you can say. This guide is about *who* and *when* to contact — *what* you advise is your call and your firm's.
