# Fed Navigator — Advisor Cheat Sheet
### Before you dial, speak in *their* words.

> **The one move that books appointments:** open the workshop report and **click the attendee's name *before* you call.** You'll instantly see their time in session, every chat they typed, every question they asked, and their evaluation comment. Now you're not "following up" — you're calling about the exact thing *they* raised. That's the difference between a voicemail and a booked meeting.

## Do this before every call — 30 seconds
1. **Click their name** in the Live attendees list to open their profile.
2. **Read their questions, chats, and comment** — that *is* what's on their mind.
3. **Open with it, in their words:** *"You asked about survivor benefits — let's put your real numbers to it."*

## Call in this order — work by heat, not top to bottom

| Call order | Who | When |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Retiring ≤ 6 months **and** asked a personal question | Within 48 hours |
| 2 | Retiring 6–12 months (including "possibly") | This week |
| 3 | Asked for cliff notes / materials — they asked *you* for something | Today, then offer a chat |
| 4 | Stayed to the end / asked multiple questions | Email now, call next |
| 5 | Everyone else who attended | Nurture list |

## What to say — steal this

> *"Hi [Name], this is [Advisor] — I led [day]'s federal retirement workshop. You asked about [their question]. That's a lot easier to walk through with your real numbers in front of us. Do you have a few minutes this week?"*

No answer? Leave a voicemail **and** text/email the same question. (Text only those who opted in — the export flags them.)

## Your week, at a glance

- **Same day** — send whatever people asked for (cliff notes, materials). That email is your opener.
- **Within 48 hours** — call your top tier, referencing their words.
- **This week** — email the rest with the workshop's rating (e.g. "rated 4.7 out of 5") and a clear ask.
- **Quarterly** — re-open past workshops: last quarter's "12 months out" is now "6 months out."

**Bottom line:** the report already told you what each person cares about. Lead with that, move fast, and the meeting books itself.
